# Adding a case study

No coding needed. About 15 minutes per case study.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- The client's name and their store link.
- One line about the project, for example "A bespoke Shopify build for a premium watch brand".
- Two or three sentences each on what the client needed, what we did and the result.
- Two screenshots of the live store:
  - **Desktop**: a full browser window, about 1440 × 900 pixels.
  - **Mobile**: a phone screen, about 390 × 844 pixels.

  To take them in Chrome: open the store, press `F12`, click the phone icon, pick "iPhone 12 Pro", then use **⋮ → Capture screenshot**. Close any newsletter pop-up before taking the screenshot.

## Steps

1. Open **https://heseven.sanity.studio** and log in.
2. Click **Case studies** in the left menu, then the **+** (Create) button.
3. **Story** tab:
   - **Client name**: for example `Waldo Watches`.
   - **Page address**: click **Generate**.
   - **Live store link**: for example `https://waldowatches.com`. Leave it empty for private or internal platforms.
   - **Headline**: one line about the project.
   - **Short summary**: two sentences. These show on the case study card.
   - **Industry** and **Platform**: pick from the lists.
   - **What we did (tags)**: tick the services we delivered.
   - **The brief**: what the client needed.
   - **What we did**: add two to four points. Each point has a short title and a sentence or two.
   - **Key features delivered**: short phrases, for example "Multi-currency checkout".
   - **The result**: how things turned out. Only write results we can stand behind.
   - **Client quote**: optional. Only use real words from the client.
4. **Images** tab:
   - Upload the **desktop screenshot** and the **mobile screenshot**.
   - Fill in **Alt text** for each, for example "Waldo Watches homepage on desktop".
5. **Listing** tab:
   - **Has its own page**: leave it on. Turn it off if the store should only appear as a card under "More recent work". Then only the name, store link and the desktop screenshot are needed.
   - **Feature at the top**: turn it on for the one case study that should appear as the big first card.
   - **Order**: lower numbers show first. The current ones use 10, 20, 30 … 90.
   - **Glow colour**: pick the colour that suits the brand.
6. Click **Publish** at the bottom right.

The website rebuilds by itself. The new case study goes live in about 3 minutes at:

- `heseven.com/pages/case-studies`, as a new card
- `heseven.com/pages/case-studies/<page address>`, as its own page

## Changing or removing one

- **To change a case study**: open it, edit, then click **Publish** again.
- **To hide one without deleting it**: open the **⋯** menu next to Publish and choose **Unpublish**.

## Good to know

- **Required fields**: anything marked in red must be filled before you can publish.
- **Numbers**: only add numbers, such as "+30% conversion", if the client has confirmed them.
- **Not live after 10 minutes?** Tell the developer. They can press **Deploy** in GitHub Actions.
